# Generativity Advantage Cheatsheet

## The Generativity Design Checklist
Use these questions to evaluate if a product feature or market policy is generative:

| Criterion | Question to Ask |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Leverage** | Does this make a difficult task significantly easier for a wide range of users? |
| **Adaptability** | Can a user use this in a way I didn't intend? |
| **Ease of Mastery** | Can a non-expert start building something useful in under 30 minutes? |
| **Accessibility** | Are there technical, financial, or legal barriers stopping a user from trying this? |
| **Transferability** | If someone builds a "cool thing," can they easily give/sell it to another user? |
| **Profitability** | Is there a clear path for a user to make $1,000/month using this? |

## Market Architecture Layers
Design your marketplace from the bottom up:

1.  **Allocation (Foundation):**
    *   Focus on **Thickness** (get more people in).
    *   Optimize **Matching** (help them find each other).
    *   Ensure **Trust & Safety** (stop bad actors).
2.  **Generation (The Engine):**
    *   Provide **Boundary Resources** (APIs, SDKs, Toolkits).
    *   Foster **Diversity** (encourage weird, niche use cases).
    *   Broaden the **Frontier** (tell everyone what the smartest users are doing).
3.  **Appropriation (Value Capture):**
    *   Set **Innovation-Friendly Pricing** (don't tax too early).
    *   Identify **Control Points** (where does the platform provide unique value?).
    *   Use **Curation** to signal quality.

## Strategic Trade-offs
When facing a conflict, use these "Generative Remedies":

| Conflict | The Problem | The Generative Remedy |
|----------|-------------|-----------------------|
| **Control vs. Generativity** | Platform safety vs. user freedom. | Use "Protector Agents" or sandboxes instead of blanket bans. |
| **Stability vs. Flexibility** | Breaking changes vs. new features. | Modular architecture and aggressive versioning. |
| **Variety vs. Utility** | Too much junk vs. useful tools. | Curation and social signaling (reviews, top charts). |
| **Originality vs. Remixing** | Plagiarism vs. building on others. | Open standards and "Remix" buttons with attribution. |
| **Openness vs. Capture** | High growth vs. high margin. | Lower fees for new categories; take profit on infrastructure. |

## Mindset Shifts for Management

| Traditional Management | Generative Management |
|-------------------------|------------------------|
| **Fail Fast** | **Patient Play** (Wait for emergent uses) |
| **Problem-Solving** | **Meta Problem-Solving** (Build tools for others) |
| **Hypothesis Testing** | **Hypothesis Development** (Observe user hacks) |
| **Customer Feedback** | **User Tinkering** (Watch what they build, not what they say) |
| **Functional Docs** | **Thick Documentation** (Explain the "Why" and the "Possible") |
